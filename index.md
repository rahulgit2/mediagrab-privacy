# Privacy Policy - MediaGrab

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

MediaGrab ("the app") is a media trimming and file-saving tool.

**Data we collect:** none. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

**Microphone:** if you use the voice recorder, the app records audio only while you have tapped Record. Recordings are saved on your device and are never uploaded or shared.

**Files:** videos, songs and images you open, trim or download stay on your device (in your Movies, Music and Pictures folders). We never upload them.

**Network use:** the app connects to the internet only to fetch the file at the link you enter.

**Payments:** the optional "Pro" one-time purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; Google tells the app only whether Pro is unlocked.

**Permissions:** internet access, notifications (to show download progress), a foreground service (so a download can finish when you leave the app), microphone access (voice recorder only), and permission to change system settings (only to set the ringtone, notification or alarm sound you choose).

**Children:** the app is not directed to children under 13.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
